C4 · Third-party appsContacts are written through a ContactsContract sync adapter, and the account is marked visible, so WhatsApp, Signal and dialers see them. This is the acceptance test for the Outlook problem.
Design rules
  • Three app screens only: Accounts, Add account, Account settings. Everything else is handled by Android.
  • Material 3, dynamic colour, edge-to-edge, predictive back. Light and dark themes come from the system.
  • No in-app view of events or contacts. No sync log in the main UI (a debug log export sits behind ⋮ → About).
  • Removing an account, whether in the app or in Settings, deletes local copies only. Nothing is deleted on iCloud.
